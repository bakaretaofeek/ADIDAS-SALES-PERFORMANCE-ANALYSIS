# ADIDAS-SALES-PERFORMANCE-ANALYSIS
# 📊 Adidas Sales Performance Dashboard | Power BI

An interactive Business Intelligence dashboard developed in **Power BI** to analyze Adidas sales performance across products, retailers, regions, and sales channels. This project transforms raw sales data into actionable business insights that support strategic decision-making and performance optimization.

---

## 📌 Project Overview

Businesses generate large volumes of sales data daily, but without effective analysis, valuable opportunities for growth and operational improvement can be overlooked.

This project analyzes Adidas sales data to evaluate revenue, profitability, product performance, retailer effectiveness, regional sales, and sales channel performance. The dashboard enables stakeholders to monitor key performance indicators (KPIs), identify trends, and make informed business decisions through interactive visualizations.

---

## 🎯 Business Objectives

The project aims to:

- Evaluate overall sales performance using key business metrics.
- Identify top-performing and underperforming product categories.
- Assess retailer contribution to revenue and profitability.
- Analyze sales performance across different regions and states.
- Compare the effectiveness of Online, In-store, and Outlet sales channels.
- Monitor sales trends over time.
- Provide actionable recommendations to improve business performance.

---

## ❓ Key Business Questions

- What is the overall sales performance during the analysis period?
- Which products generate the highest revenue and profit?
- Which retailers contribute the most to business growth?
- Which regions present the strongest market opportunities?
- Which sales channels are the most profitable?
- How has sales performance changed over time?
- What strategic actions can improve revenue and profitability?

## 🔍 Key Insights

### Product Performance

- Men's Street Footwear emerged as the highest revenue-generating product category.
- The top three product categories contributed the majority of total revenue.
- Women's Athletic Footwear presented opportunities for increased market penetration.

### Retailer Performance

- West Gear generated the highest revenue.
- Sports Direct achieved the highest profit margin among retailers.
- Revenue concentration among a few retailers highlights the need for diversification.

### Regional Performance

- The West region generated the highest revenue.
- The Midwest achieved the strongest profit margin.
- Regional performance varied significantly, indicating opportunities for targeted investment.

### Sales Channel Performance

- Online sales generated the highest revenue.
- In-store sales delivered the highest profitability.
- Outlet sales maintained balanced revenue and profitability.

### Trend Analysis

- Revenue increased significantly between 2020 and 2021.
- Q3 2021 recorded the strongest sales performance.
- Sales trends indicate sustainable business growth.

## 💡 Business Recommendations

- Increase investment in high-performing product categories.

- Strengthen partnerships with top-performing retailers.
- Expand successful products into high-growth regions.
- Leverage seasonal demand patterns for inventory planning.
- Reduce dependency on a small number of retailers by expanding market reach.
